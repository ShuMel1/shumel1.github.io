Meow Cozy Café — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Meow Cozy Café: Block Puzzle ("the game") is made by Shushan Meliksetyan. This page explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, hearts, café items and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent first, and on iPhone it asks for permission before using the advertising identifier. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings, or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example which levels are hard and when players stop playing) so it can be improved. This uses an app instance identifier and anonymous usage data, not your name or contact details.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is only shared with the Google services above, for the purposes described.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the game's settings.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's privacy settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete the progress saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
