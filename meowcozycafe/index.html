A relaxing block puzzle with cats. Fit the blocks, clear lines, and build your own cozy cat café.

Support

Found a bug, lost your progress, or have an idea? Write to shushmeliqsetyan@gmail.com and include your phone model. We usually answer within 2 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free? Yes. It's free to play and supported by ads. Watching an ad is always your choice, except a short ad between some games.

Where is my progress saved? On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Tap "Privacy" on the home screen (shown where the law requires it), or change ad tracking in your phone's settings.
