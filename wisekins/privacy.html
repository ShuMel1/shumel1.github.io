🦉 Wisekins — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Wisekins is a language-learning app for children. We built it to be safe and private by design. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Wisekins has no accounts, no login, no sign-up, and no servers of its own.

  • A child's profile name, chosen languages, learning progress, streaks and daily goals are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • All words, pictures, recordings and lessons come inside the app. Nothing is downloaded from us.
  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.
  • There is no usage analytics: we do not record which games are played.

Support ID

Each install has a random code such as WISE-7K4P-92QX, shown in Settings → Support ID. It is made on your device and contains no personal information. It is used as the anonymous customer id for subscriptions (see below). If you need help, you can send it to us so we can find your subscription.

Microphone and the speaking game

Wisekins includes a speaking game where a child says a word out loud and the app checks the pronunciation. This is the only part of the app that uses the microphone.

  • The microphone is used only while the speaking game is open, and only after the child taps the microphone button. The app never listens in the background.
  • Your device asks for microphone permission the first time it is needed. If you decline, the rest of the app continues to work normally.
  • We never receive your child's voice. Audio is not recorded to a file, is not stored, and is never sent to us.
  • Speech is turned into text by your device's own speech recognition — the same system used for dictation on the keyboard. This happens on the device whenever the device supports it.
  • If on-device recognition is unavailable for the chosen language, your device may send the audio to Apple's speech recognition service on iOS, or Google's on Android, to convert it to text. This is handled by the operating system under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies. You can avoid this entirely by not using the speaking game.
  • The app only receives the recognized text, compares it to the expected word on the device, and discards it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Wisekins offers optional subscriptions (Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly) that unlock Games, Avatars, and Daily Adventure after a free trial. Learning stays free forever, with or without a subscription.

  • All payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Wisekins never sees, receives, or stores your payment card details.
  • We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to check subscription status. RevenueCat receives the random Support ID together with your purchase and subscription status from the App Store or Play Store. It does not receive your name, email address, payment details, or anything about your child's learning.
  • Because Wisekins is built for children, every purchase screen is protected by a parental gate — a simple typed math question shown before any subscription screen, so a child cannot start a purchase without an adult.

Children's privacy

Wisekins is designed for children and is compliant with children's privacy laws including COPPA and GDPR-K. We collect no personal information about your child. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking anywhere in the app.

Third parties

We do not share, rent, or sell any information to anyone. The app uses only two outside services, neither of which is used for advertising or tracking:

  • RevenueCat, to manage subscription purchases — see "Subscriptions and purchases" above for exactly what it receives.
  • Your device's built-in speech recognition, used only in the speaking game as described above.

Data retention and deletion

All of your child's data stays on the device. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Older versions of the app

Older versions sent an anonymous usage count to our server and, only if a parent turned it on, kept a backup copy of progress there for moving to another device. That server is being shut down, and everything stored on it will be permanently deleted.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
